Overview
Instead is revolutionizing the tax industry as a cutting-edge platform that facilitates collaboration between taxpayers and tax professionals to maximize tax savings. This technical assessment evaluates your ability to design a robust annotation system for U.S. tax forms—a core component of our platform's functionality.


Technical test
Define a new data structure for how to annotate fields/boxes on a U.S. tax form.

Deliver your structure as JSON, XML, or any other format of your choice, including classes in any popular programming language.

Include written documentation outlining your specification.

Someone should be able to annotate a tax form according to your spec and build an application that can print 
values on top of the forms within each box using the annotation and their proprietary code.

Your spec should include positioning, any formatting needed and how to reference a specific value from a deeply nested data set.

Include a video walkthrough with your deliverable, max 5 minutes long. You can use Loom or any other screen recording app.

You will be assessed based on the scope you have accounted for, cleanliness of your deliverable and quality of your walkthrough. You are encouraged to point out any potential enhancements that you could envision adding in the future and to describe the decision you made."
